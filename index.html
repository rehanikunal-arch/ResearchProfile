Higher Degree Research Courses and Pathways

Explore research-focused postgraduate pathways combining advanced study, research training and supervised research, with opportunities to progress towards doctoral research.

ICT Research

Master of Information and Communications Technology (Research)

MICTres

Advanced ICT coursework followed by supervised research training, designed to build research capability in the ICT discipline.

  • Two and a half years full-time.
  • Combines advanced ICT study with supervised research training.
  • Supports research projects within the University or with external organisations.
  • Useful pathway towards Higher Degree Research and PhD study.
  • Relevant to computing, systems, data, cloud, networks and related ICT research.

AI Research

Master of Artificial Intelligence (Research)

MAIres

Advanced Artificial Intelligence study followed by supervised AI research, building both technical foundations and discipline-specific research skills.

  • Two and a half years full-time, with part-time study also available where applicable.
  • Includes advanced AI, machine learning, natural language processing and AI ethics.
  • Includes a full-time-equivalent year of supervised AI research training.
  • Research may be undertaken within the University or with external industry partners.
  • Provides preparation for PhD research and AI-focused research careers.

Research Masters

Master of Research

MRes

A broad research-training pathway combining structured research preparation with an independently supervised Higher Degree Research project.

  • Stage 1 develops research methods, disciplinary depth and research communication.
  • Stage 2 centres on a supervised Higher Degree Research project.
  • Includes a substantial thesis or equivalent research output.
  • Supports research across a broad range of CDMS-related disciplines.
  • Designed as strong preparation for PhD study and research careers.
